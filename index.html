Why PawZa Exists

PawZa started after we adopted Freddie, our first dog and a retired greyhound.

We wanted to help him socialise and meet other dogs, but finding local group walks was harder than expected. Facebook groups were useful but fragmented, and it was difficult to discover walks nearby.

PawZa was created to make local dog walks easier to find, join and organise.

Features

Discover nearby walks
Create group walks
Walk chat
Open meeting points in Maps
Meet local dog owners
Built specifically for dog walks

How It Works

  1. Create your profile
  2. Add your dog
  3. Find or create a walk
  4. Meet local dog owners
